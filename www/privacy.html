Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version

DAD. doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved jokes, your groan-meter ratings and your daily-notification setting are stored only on your device.
  • They are removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily joke, the notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Fresh jokes from the internet

To add variety, the app may request a random joke from icanhazdadjoke.com, a free public joke service. That request contains no personal information. Like any website, that service may see your device's IP address. Its own privacy terms apply.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.
